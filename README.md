**Making Honey More Transparent, Trusted & Smarter**
*"From the Beekeeper's Hive to the Consumer's Home."*

Domain: Agriculture & Rural Development · Category: Software Edition
**Result: Cleared the PPT screening round, advanced to Round 2**

## Problem

The traditional honey supply chain is a blind, unverified loop. Consumers have no
way to check a honey batch's origin or purity, small beekeepers lack affordable
tools to monitor hive health, and KVIC (which runs the national Honey Mission)
has no unified, real-time view of production across the country. Existing
solutions — lab testing, IoT hive sensors, blockchain traceability pilots — each
solve one piece, but none connect beekeeper, lab, KVIC and consumer in a single
system.

## Our Idea

BeeTrust AI gives every honey batch a **Digital Honey Passport**: one digital
record covering origin, hive data, harvest date, lab results, certification and
movement history. An AI engine flags hive risk and forecasts yield from that
data, and consumers verify a batch's full history with a single QR scan.

**Flow:** Beekeeper registers hive → records batch → AI analyses risk/yield →
Digital Passport generated → consumer scans QR → verified info displayed.

## Tech Stack (proposed)

- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Backend:** Python
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / MongoDB
- **AI/ML:** Python, Scikit-learn (hive risk & yield analysis)
- **Traceability/Security:** Hyperledger Fabric, JWT auth
